Short summary

Pip is a poor orphan who lives with his sister and her husband, Joe, a kind blacksmith. One day, Pip helps an escaped convict called Magwitch. Later, he meets the rich Miss Havisham and falls in love with her adopted daughter, Estella.

A secret benefactor gives Pip money to become a gentleman in London. Pip believes that Miss Havisham is helping him, but his real benefactor is Magwitch. Estella marries the cruel Bentley Drummle. Pip becomes ashamed of his poor background but eventually regrets his behaviour.

In the end, Pip learns that friendship, kindness and loyalty are more important than money and social status.
